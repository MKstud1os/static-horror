    How to Play

    A research facility of 20 rooms has been taken over by a living signal. You have no weapons. Explore, listen, hide, and work together.

    W A S DmoveMouselookShiftsprint (uses stamina)Ctrl or Ccrouch (quieter) Einteract, read a note, hide in a locker, revive a teammateFflashlight QEMP pulse: stuns STATIC for a few seconds if it is in front of you and close. Long cooldown.Escpause

    Trace the signal in the Security Office, reset both breakers to open the Signal Chamber gate, then decide at the terminal. Notes hint at a secret ending. STATIC hears running, light and noisy equipment, and notices anyone standing still. Corruption rises over time and with every capture. At 100% you lose.
